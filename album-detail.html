About the Artist

AC/DC is an Australian rock band formed in Sydney in 1973 by Scottish-born brothers Malcolm and Angus Young. They are considered pioneers of hard rock and heavy metal, known for their high-energy performances and iconic riffs. With over 200 million records sold worldwide, AC/DC has left an indelible mark on the music industry and continues to influence generations of musicians.
